# FAQ content approval queue

This is an internal approval queue, not public copy.

## Proposed questions requiring Franchise Development and legal approval

- What happens after I submit a franchise inquiry?
  - Source to validate: Contact and Process pages.
  - Keep the inquiry-not-application, territory-reservation, and offer boundaries intact.

- How are operating experience and leadership background reviewed?
  - Source to validate: Candidate Profile.
  - Do not add years of experience, role requirements, or subjective personality criteria.

- When and how is FDD information provided?
  - Source to validate: gated FDD and process flow.
  - Do not imply public FDD access or availability before the approved process.

- How do capital qualification and total startup investment differ?
  - Source to validate: governed financial disclosure.
  - Do not publish financial figures, fees, or performance implications without current placement approval.

- How do Budda's support and operator responsibility work together?
  - Source to validate: Opportunity support runway.
  - Confirm every support promise with its owning team.
