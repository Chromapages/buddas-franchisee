# Supplies Bakery Counter design mode

The authenticated Supplies experience uses Budda’s functional Bakery Counter mode:

- Canvas: Budda’s Cream `#FFF8E8`
- Dense functional surfaces: white
- Structure and primary actions: Dark Teal `#1C5F56`
- Recognition and focus: Base Teal `#54BFA5`
- Body and operational metadata: Cocoa Brown `#5A3A1F`
- Rare detail only: Island Gold `#E9C559`
- Urgency only: Sunset Orange `#D36200`

Poppins carries page titles, section headings, product names, prices, and primary actions. DM Sans carries descriptions, SKU/pack metadata, status, navigation, form fields, and utilities.

Mobile uses 20px gutters, 16px internal rhythm, 12–16px surface radii, white product rows, and restrained dividers. Persistent purchasing docks are white functional surfaces with a Dark Teal structural edge; their Add and View Cart actions use Dark Teal with Cream text. Base Teal never carries white body text. No botanical, arc, campaign, food-promotion, or normal-action Orange treatment belongs in Supplies.
